Privacy Policy — 0xdps labs

Last Updated: December 2025

Introduction

0xDPS is an independent developer ecosystem that creates tools, extensions, APIs, and utilities for software developers. This Privacy Policy explains our general data-handling principles and links to the individual policies for each product.

By using any 0xDPS product, you agree to the individual privacy policy for that product and this general policy.

What Information We Collect (General)

Across the ecosystem, we may collect limited data solely for functional or analytical purposes, depending on the product. This may include:

  • Basic technical data (IP, device info, logs)
  • Error logs or crash reports
  • Authentication-related identifiers (if applicable)
  • Data required for using APIs or extensions

We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties. Each product declares its own specific behavior.

What We Do Not Collect

Unless explicitly required:

  • We do not collect personal identity information
  • We do not track users across websites
  • We do not store private content, code, or messages
  • We do not monetize or analyze user data
