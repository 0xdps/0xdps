Pinboard GPT Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

Overview

Pinboard GPT is a browser extension that allows users to pin, organize, and save ChatGPT messages for later reference.

Data Collected

Local-Only Storage

All your pinned data stays on your device:

  • Pinned messages (only those you manually select)
  • Categories and tags you create
  • User preferences and settings
No data is sent to any external server unless you explicitly choose to sync via your own storage account (future feature).

Data Not Collected

  • ChatGPT account information
  • Conversations stored on OpenAI servers
  • Personal identifiers or email
  • General browser activity
  • Messages you did not manually pin

How We Protect Your Data

We do not access content outside the pinned messages you manually select. Pinboard GPT only reads the specific messages you choose to pin and stores them locally in your browser.

Third-Party Services

None. Pinboard GPT operates independently without relying on external analytics or servers.

Future Sync Feature

If Pinboard GPT adds a sync feature in the future, users will have the option to store data in their own cloud accounts (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox). A separate privacy disclosure will be provided at that time.
